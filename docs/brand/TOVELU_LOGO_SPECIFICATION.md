# Tovelu Brand Identity: The Homeostasis Ring Specification

> **Constitutional Authority**: Articles 3, 5, 16, 39, 41, 95, 105 & 106.  
> **Brand Mark**: The Homeostasis Ring (The Living Equilibrium).  
> **Tagline**: Towards Better Health.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of The Homeostasis Ring

The Homeostasis Ring is composed of two asymmetric, breathing arcs in dynamic balance around a central living core:
1. **Arc 1 (The Living Science Arc)**: Top-right arc representing objective biomedical science, artificial intelligence, and proactive vitality.
2. **Arc 2 (The Human Reality Arc)**: Bottom-left arc representing the human biological baseline, real-world living context, and longitudinal metrics.
3. **The Living Core**: The central circular point representing the individual human at the center of intelligence.

---

## 2. The Universal Color & Contrast Rule (The Founder Law)

> **Rule**: Arc 2 MUST NOT rely on ambiguous CSS text inheritance. Its color is strictly governed by the surface luminance and ambient background:

| Background Environment | Background Hex Range | Arc 1 (Living Science) | Arc 2 (Human Reality) | The Living Core |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Light Surfaces** | `#FFFFFF` to `#E2E8F0`<br>*(White, Light Gray, Cream, Cotton)* | **Teal/Emerald Gradient**<br>(`#0D9488` $\rightarrow$ `#10B981`) | **Pure Obsidian Black**<br>(`#090D14` • 100% Solid) | **Teal/Emerald Gradient** |
| **Dark Surfaces** | `#000000` to `#1E293B`<br>*(Black, Obsidian, Dark Navy, Slate)* | **Teal/Emerald Gradient**<br>(`#0D9488` $\rightarrow$ `#10B981`) | **Pure Crisp White**<br>(`#FFFFFF` • 100% Solid) | **Teal/Emerald Gradient** |
| **Brand / Colored Surfaces** | `#0D9488` or Vibrant Backgrounds | **Pure Solid White**<br>(`#FFFFFF`) | **Pure Solid White**<br>(`#FFFFFF` • 85% Opacity) | **Pure Solid White** |
| **1-Color Print / Fax / Thermal** | Monochrome Print | **Pure Solid Black**<br>(`#000000`) | **Pure Solid Black**<br>(`#000000` • 85% Density) | **Pure Solid Black** |

## 3. Clear Space & Minimum Sizing

- **Clear Space**: Always maintain clear space around the mark equal to at least half the radius of the outer ring ($0.5R$).
- **Minimum Digital Size**: $16 \times 16\text{px}$ (Micro Favicon / Status Complication).
- **Minimum Print Size**: $8\text{mm} \times 8\text{mm}$ (Packaging / Prescription Stamp).
