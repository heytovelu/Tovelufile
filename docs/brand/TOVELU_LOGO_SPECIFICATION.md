$$\text{FULL LOGO} = \mathbf{THE \; HOMEOSTASIS \; RING} \; + \; \mathbf{T \; O \; V \; E \; L \; U}$$

- **No Tagline**: Never attach a secondary tagline to the official logo lockup. The icon and name stand sovereign, confident, and clutter-free (Apple, Nike, Sony standard).
- **Wordmark Typography**:

  - **Geometry**: The **O** is a geometrically perfect circle mirroring the curvature of The Homeostasis Ring.
