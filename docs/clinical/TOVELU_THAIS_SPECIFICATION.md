## 2. Adaptive Motivation & Anti-Quit Engine

- **Dynamic Intensity Scaling**: If a user's nightly check-in flags high emotional exhaustion or physical fatigue, THAIS automatically recalibrates the next day's tasks to be gentler (e.g. swaps an intense workout for a restorative 15-minute walk and adds 15g healthy fats to support brain neurotransmitters).
- **Zero Guilt Protocol**: Missing a task or over-eating never results in red failure badges. THAIS rebalances the math forward without judgment.

## 3. Active Clinical Inquiry in Nightly Check-In (Closing Uncertainty)

- When THAIS detects a potential symptom or pattern during the day but lacks 100% confidence, it dynamically injects **ONE precision confirmation question** into Level 2 of that night's 1-minute survey:
  - *Example*: *"We noticed your lunch had higher sodium than usual. Did you experience any slight puffiness in your fingers or extra thirst this afternoon?"*
  - Answering this single micro-question immediately resolves the diagnostic uncertainty and updates the user's clinical risk profile.

## 4. The "Swipe to Complete" Haptic Interaction Standard

- **No Cheap Checkboxes**: Users NEVER click a small checkbox to mark a task or meal finished.
- **Swipe-to-Action Mechanism**:
  - Every task and meal has a smooth, tactile **Swipe Slider** (similar to "Slide to Unlock" on iOS).
  - **Zero Accidental Clicks**: Prevents accidental taps while scrolling on mobile.
  - **Dopamine Victory Release**: The physical sensation of sliding a bar across the screen gives an immediate neurological rush of accomplishment and closes the habit loop.
